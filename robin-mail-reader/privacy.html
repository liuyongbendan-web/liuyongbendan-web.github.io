Privacy Policy

Robin Mail Reader is a personal tool used only by its owner to access the owner's own Gmail account.

  • Gmail data is accessed through the Gmail API only on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sent to, stored on, or shared with any third-party server.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account "Third-party access" page.
  • The app does not collect analytics, and does not sell or transfer data.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
